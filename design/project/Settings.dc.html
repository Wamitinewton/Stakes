How Stakes handles your data

Stakes runs on Atlassian Forge and makes no calls outside Atlassian. Customer and deal data stays in Forge storage and the Teamwork Graph. Owner emails from a CSV are turned into Atlassian account IDs on import and then discarded.

Every read runs as the person viewing, so nobody sees a deal, ticket or page they couldn't open in Jira. Every change to an issue needs a confirmation and is posted as the person who approved it. Logs hold IDs and counts only, never deal values, customer names or ticket text.

Removing demo data or disconnecting the connector deletes that data.
